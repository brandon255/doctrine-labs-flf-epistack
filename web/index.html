How was this computed?

Each excerpt becomes a claim. Claims that share a source URL become one document. Documents that trace to the same underlying event become one lineage. The number that matters is the lineage count.

Inflation factor = excerpts ÷ lineages. A 6× factor means your 20 cited sources trace back to about 3 independent observations.

How it works

    
Three levels of independence.

    

      Counting citations overstates what you know. Counting documents still overstates it,
      because two judges watching the same debate are not two independent witnesses.
    


    

      

        
1

        
Claim

        
One excerpt per citation. The loud unit.

        
Every quotation counts as a source.

      

      

        
2

        
Document

        
One PDF, one post. The medium unit.

        
Two accounts of one event look independent.

      

      

        
3

        
Lineage

        
One underlying event. The conservative unit.

        
The headline number. Treat this as your real source count.
